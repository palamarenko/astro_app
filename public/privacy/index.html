Privacy Policy

Iruna  ·  Last updated: May 14, 2025

This Privacy Policy describes how Iruna ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and protects information when you use our mobile application available on Android and iOS.

1. Information We Collect

We collect only the information you voluntarily provide in order to deliver personalized astrological content:

  • Date of birth — used to generate your horoscope and compatibility readings.
  • Place of birth — used for astrological chart calculations.

This information is stored locally on your device and is not transmitted to our servers or shared with third parties, except as described below.

2. Push Notifications

With your permission, we send push notifications (e.g., daily horoscope reminders). We use Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) by Google to deliver these messages. FCM may process a device token to route notifications to your device. You can disable notifications at any time in your device settings.

3. Advertising

The app displays ads provided by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and use your device's advertising identifier (GAID on Android, IDFA on iOS) and other non-personal technical data to show relevant ads. For more information, see Google's Privacy Policy.

You can opt out of personalized advertising in your device settings:

  • Android: Settings → Google → Ads → Opt out of Ads Personalization.
  • iOS: Settings → Privacy → Tracking → disable tracking for Iruna.

4. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services, each governed by their own privacy policies:

  • Firebase (Google) — push notifications and analytics.
  • Google AdMob — in-app advertising.

5. Data Retention

Your birth date and place of birth are stored locally on your device. You can delete them at any time by clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app.

6. Children's Privacy

Iruna is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe your child has provided us with personal data, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected by the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
